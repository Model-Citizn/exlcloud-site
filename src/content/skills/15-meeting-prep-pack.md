## What it does

Meeting Prep Pack turns the month's financial results, or a finished board paper, into a one-page brief you can scan in five minutes before walking into the room: what to say first, what went well, what didn't and what you plan to do about it, and the questions you are likely to be asked.

## When to use it

- Before a board, investor or lender meeting.
- After Skill 07 has produced the board paper, which is the ideal source material.

## How it works

1. **Read the source:** the board paper or the underlying financial data.
2. **Draft the brief:**
   - a one-sentence headline;
   - the top three wins and top three challenges in dollars and percent, each challenge with a mitigation;
   - a watch list of items that need another month of data;
   - 8 to 10 anticipated questions with suggested responses, drawn from budget misses over 10%, cash, receivables, headcount, one-offs, outlook, capex, covenants, prior-year comparison and trend breaks;
   - risk flags rated high, medium or low.
3. **Suggest an agenda:** a ten-minute flow from headline to revenue, margins and costs, cash and balance sheet, risks and outlook, then decisions.
4. **Check the context.** Claude asks about board focus, outstanding actions from the last meeting, and any confidential items not reflected in the numbers.

## What you get

A single scannable page built around three key messages, written to the same commentary standards as the other narrative skills.

## Good to know

- Text output only. It doesn't write board papers (Skill 07) or commentary (Skill 06), and it doesn't modify workbooks.
- Every challenge is paired with a mitigation.
- It notes whether the chair should be briefed before the meeting.
- The emphasis adapts to the audience: investors focus on growth and valuation, lenders on cash and covenants.
